HOW IT WORKS
Sky brightness starts from the Bortle class or meter reading, brightens toward the horizon with airmass, then adds moonlight (Krisciunas & Schaefer 1991 model) and twilight from the Sun's depth below the horizon. The local sky brightness converts to a naked-eye limiting magnitude with the standard SQM-to-NELM formula, adjusted for pupil size by age and for dark adaptation. Each object's magnitude is dimmed by atmospheric extinction for its altitude and compared against that limit.

VERDICTS
Naked eye: at least 0.5 magnitude brighter than the limit. Borderline: within 0.5, likely needs averted vision. Binoculars: within the reach of the aperture you set. Results are estimates; experienced observers often beat them, and local glare from a nearby streetlight is not modeled.
